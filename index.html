What is Shilajit?

Shilajit is a naturally occurring mineral-rich substance traditionally found in mountainous regions, including the Himalayas. It develops over long periods through the breakdown of organic plant material and contains various minerals and organic compounds.

Shilajit has been used in traditional Ayurvedic practices for general wellness and vitality. One of its well-known components is fulvic acid, which is often studied for its biological properties.

Today, Shilajit is available in different forms, including resin, powder, capsules and formulated products.
